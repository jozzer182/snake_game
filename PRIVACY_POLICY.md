# Privacy Policy for Fidi - Snake Game

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 13, 2025

## Introduction

Fidi - Snake Game ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our mobile application.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

Fidi is a completely offline game that operates entirely on your device. We respect your privacy and have designed the app to function without requiring any personal information.

## Local Data Storage

The app uses local storage on your device only to save:

- **Game Settings**: Your preferred snake color, background theme, and game speed
- **High Scores**: Your best scores and recent game results
- **Game State**: Current game progress when paused

This data is stored locally on your device using Flutter's `shared_preferences` plugin and **never leaves your device**. It is not transmitted to any servers or third parties.

## No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Tracking tools
- Cookies
- User identification systems

## No Internet Connection Required

Fidi operates completely offline. The app does not:
- Connect to the internet during gameplay
- Send any data to external servers
- Download content from remote sources
- Communicate with third-party services

## External Links

The app contains a link to the developer's website (https://zarabanda-dev.web.app/) in the main menu footer. This link is purely informational and optional. Clicking it will open your device's web browser. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

## Children's Privacy

Fidi is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, we comply with children's privacy protection laws such as COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

## Data Security

All game data is stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's security features. We do not have access to this data.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by:
1. Uninstalling the app from your device
2. Clearing the app's data through your device settings

This will remove all saved settings and scores.

## Third-Party Services

Fidi uses the following Flutter packages that operate locally on your device:
- **flame**: Game engine for rendering graphics
- **shared_preferences**: Local data persistence
- **url_launcher**: Opens external links in your browser (only when you click the developer link)

None of these packages collect or transmit personal data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data for you to access, modify, or delete from our servers. All data resides on your device under your control.
